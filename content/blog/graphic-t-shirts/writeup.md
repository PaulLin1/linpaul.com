**(1, 1):** A gift from my girlfriend after I tried it on at a vintage clothing shop. I think it caught my eye because I was listening to Magnolia Electric Co. a lot at the time and the design reminded me of the that. The shirt features an owl print with a moon cycle by Aerie Design and dates to 1980. Printed on a Made in USA Hanes Beefy.

**(2, 1):** A crest graphic t-shirt by Garni & Co. Below the crest theres text that says style of art in a hyeroglific type font. An odd detail is the tag is blank with the detials printed on the back of the shirt, making it look like you're wearing the shirt inside out. Found at a Second Street in Koenji.

**(3, 1):** A Study at Stow Belle and Sebastian reprint I bought at the 20th anniversary Belle and Sebastian concert. This shirt was a grail for like a year, and when I saw it at the merch booth I knew I had to buy it. The fit is a bit odd and I had to bleach away the weird blue color of the shirt so now it's violently bright. It's not a shirt I wear a lot, but the design is still one of my favorites.

**(1, 2):** The first shirt in my collection, a 90's Born to Run t-shirt printed on AllSport Max Weight Made in USA. Despite being an XL and heavily trashed now, this will always be my favorite shirt. This album cover is the perfect graphic. Purchased at a vintage shop in NYC in 2023.

**(2, 2):** Go for the Gold the Soloist t-shirt. TAKAHIROMIYASHITA The Soloist is another brand  by the founder of Number Nine. The pocket tee also has a number 13 print on the back. Like the Garni & Co tee, the is a label on the outside of the shirt, this time near the hem.

**(3, 2):** Club 19 vintage t printed on a Screen Stars Best Made in USA. The back has a graphic saying "WHERE THE EXCITEMENT BEGINS...". I always feel a little weird wearing it so I don't. But I really like the front graphic. The first iteration of my personal website had a title page using the 2 block graphic.

**(1, 3):** 2012 White Castle T.

**(2, 3):** A shirt with a drawing by a Japanese artist found in the same Second Street as the Garni & Co. The graphic is quite odd. I get a lot of weird looks when I wear it.

**(3, 3):** Belle & Sebastian t shirt. Printed on a Fruit of the Loom T shirt, I am assuming it is a fan bootleg. Nonetheless, still a favorite in my collection. I love the obnoxious looking & and the red on navy.

**(1, 4):** 2002 PRS Dragon T. A promotinnal t shirt for the ultra expensive dragon line of guitars by PRS. The colors pop over the very black grey blank. This t shirt fits me perfectly.

**(2, 4):** Acne Studios You First Unisex T Shirt. The You First sweatshirt became a grail after I saw Bladee wear it. The one he had might be the stupidest looking graphic ever, but something about it stuck with me throughout high school and college. When I saw this shirt, with a better graphic, on Depop, I had to buy it.

**(3, 4):** Calvin Klein Hoodie. Not a t shirt, but one of my favorite graphics in my collection. Unfortunately, it has a disgusting red Calvin Klein logo on the back that I've been meaning to dye to match the rest of the hoodie.
