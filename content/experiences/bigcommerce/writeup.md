During the summer of 2024, I worked as a Software Engineering Intern at BigCommerce, an e-commerce platform used by thousands of online stores. I joined a team responsible for internal cloud infrastructure. Over the course of the internship, I got hands-on experience working with AWS to build pipelines for large-scale data transfers. I mainly worked with transfers between DynamoDB and RDS with clear logging. I learned how to work with API's and system boundaries. Although the work felt a bit monotonous at times, I realized how important efficiency is for scaling a large company. We worked in an 'assembly line' team where each person worked on a small section of a large project. For my role, I was given an architecture diagram and had to make the pipeline and hand it off to testers. This allowed me to ship and be a part of many projects throughout the course of my internship. This fast, iterative approach taught me how to work under a tight timeline. Another interesting part of the internship was that we were encouraged to adopt AI tools and code generation to further improve speed. This experience helped me learn how to effectively use these tools, which I now use every day.
