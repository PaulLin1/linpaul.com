During the summer of 2024, I worked as a Software Engineering Intern at BigCommerce, an e-commerce platform used by thousands of online stores. I joined a team responsible for internal cloud infrastructure. Over the course of the internship, I got hands-on experience working with AWS building pipelines for large scale data transfers. Although the work felt a bit monotonous at times, I realized how important efficiency is for scaling a large scale companies. We worked in an 'assembly line' team where each person worked on a small section of a large project. I was given an architecture diagram and had to make the pipeline and hand it off to testers. This fast approach taught me how to work under a tight timeline. Additionally, we were suggsested to adopt AI tools and code generation to further improve speed. I think helped me learn how to effectively use these tools, which I now use a lot.
