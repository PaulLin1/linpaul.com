During the summer of 2024, I worked as a Software Engineering Intern at BigCommerce, an e-commerce platform used by thousands of online stores. I joined a team responsible for internal tooling and storefront infrastructure, shipping features that were used across the platform.

Over the course of the internship, I got hands-on experience working in a large, mature codebase, writing tests, and going through code review with senior engineers. I also presented my work at the end-of-summer intern showcase.
