Starting in 2025 and continuing through 2026, I worked as a research assistant in the ECODE Lab, contributing to ongoing projects at the intersection of evolutionary computation and AI interpretability.

My work involved building tooling to analyze and visualize model internals, running experiments, and collaborating with graduate students and faculty on research that fed directly into coursework and publications.
