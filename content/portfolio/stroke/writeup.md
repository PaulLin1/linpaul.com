A query-efficient score-based black-box adversarial attack that constructs perturbations from simple oriented rectangular occlusions.

## Background

During my senior year at Michigan State University, I worked with PhD candidate Ankit Gupta on many projects related to image detection and evolutionary computing. We first got connected when I took a graduate course on algorithms taught by his lab's PI, Dr. Dolson, and she mentioned that Ankit was working on problems that I was interested in. At the time, I researched a lot about the work of Jeff Clune, an alumnus of Michigan State University, in evolutionary computing for machine learning tasks. Ankit already had a backlog of research problems and gladly took me in as a research assistant.

## Process

Ankit was working on a suite of algorithms to fool image classification models. He had many ideas and offered to let me work on ones I found interesting. I picked one and worked on the entire pipeline from ideation to algorithm development to data collection. I learned a lot about the research process and how to compare results fairly.
